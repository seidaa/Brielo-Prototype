# Brio — Brand Brief

---

## What Is Brio?

Brio is a mobile-first social discovery app that makes it frictionless to do things with people in real life. Not plans that never happen. Not group chats that go nowhere. Actual moves — things you post, people join, and you all show up for.

Brio is built on a simple loop:

> **Make a Move → People join → You go → Build your Circle**

---

## Positioning

**Category:** Social discovery / spontaneous IRL coordination

**For:** People who want to do more outside the screen — who are already thinking about going to the gym, grabbing coffee, playing pickup, or checking out a spot — and want a way to bring someone else into it without the friction.

**Against:** Endless group chats, event apps that feel transactional, and social media that substitutes watching for doing.

**What Brio is not:** A dating app. A ticketing app. An events calendar. A forum. A feed of content. Brio is a coordination layer for doing real things.

## User Language

"Mover" is **not** a generic noun for all users. It's reserved for badge and status contexts only.

**Natural language for users:**
- people
- hosts
- attendees
- Circle members
- people in your Circle
- people you've moved with

**"Mover" is allowed only in badge / status context:**
- Early Mover *(badge)*
- Trusted Mover *(badge)*
- Local Mover *(profile label, higher-level users only)*
- Top Mover *(leaderboard status)*
- Would Move Again *(feedback label)*

**Never write:**
- ~~"Find Movers nearby"~~ → "Find people nearby"
- ~~"Message other Movers"~~ → "Message attendees"
- ~~"Movers are attending"~~ → "4 people going"
- ~~"Add this Mover"~~ → "Add to Circle"

## The Circle — Core Social Model

A Circle is not a group, a feed, or a follower count. It's a **personal trust graph** built one Move at a time.

Three layers:

| Layer | Description |
|---|---|
| **My Circle** | People you've moved with and explicitly added. Your trusted people. |
| **Would Move Again** | People from past Moves who left a good impression. On deck. |
| **Recent Move Connections** | People from recent Moves. New — not yet trusted, not yet ignored. |

Circles are private. Nothing is public. No follower counts. No mutual counts shown to others.

## Content Tone of Voice

**Principles:**

1. **Short, direct, active.** Every label is a verb or a noun that implies action. Nothing passive.
2. **Warm, not hype.** Brio doesn't oversell. "Good vibes" is a chip, not a headline. "I'm In" is enough.
3. **Honest about safety.** Negative feedback (Felt off, No-show, Report) is handled privately. Never shown publicly. Confirmed with "Thanks. We'll review this." — not a bureaucratic success toast.
4. **No startup speak.** No "connect with", "discover experiences", "unlock social potential." Real words. Real city.

**Brio writes like this:**
> "What's the move?"
> "You're in. Move Chat is open."
> "Who showed up?"
> "Your Circle = your people."
> "Move again?"

**Brio does not write like this:**
> ~~"Connect with like-minded individuals in your area"~~
> ~~"Discover curated experiences near you"~~
> ~~"Empower your social life"~~

## User Levels & Status

Users earn XP and level up through moves — attending, hosting, and building their Circle. Levels are visible but not competitive. They signal trust and presence, not clout.

**Badge examples (correct use of "Mover"):**
- Early Mover
- Shows Up
- Trusted Mover
- Local Mover *(Lv 7+)*

**XP milestones are private** — shown only on the user's own profile.

---

## Safety Model

Brio is not anonymous but is semi-private by design.

- Negative person feedback (Felt off / No-show / Report) is **never shown publicly**
- Reports are private, reviewed internally
- Report options are specific: No-show / Creepy behavior / Harassment / Fake Move / Spam/scam / Other
- Confirmation: *"Thanks. We'll review this."* — brief, human, not corporate
