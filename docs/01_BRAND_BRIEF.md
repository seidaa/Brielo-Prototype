# Brielo Brand Brief

## App Name

Brielo

Pronunciation: BREE-loh

Previous names/codenames:

* Rally
* Brio

Do not use Rally or Brio as user-facing names going forward.

## Brand Stack

* App name: Brielo
* Primary tagline: Live More
* Core action phrase: Make a Move
* Secondary marketing line: Less scrolling. More doing.
* Supporting product line: Find people to do things with — once, weekly, or whenever you're down.

## Brand Meaning

Brielo evolved from Brio, which means energy, life, spirit, enthusiasm, and liveliness. Brielo should feel more ownable while keeping the same energy/life/social momentum meaning.

## Brand Personality

Brielo should feel:

* modern
* warm
* social
* energetic
* youthful
* simple
* lifestyle-focused

Avoid:

* dating app feel
* professional networking feel
* Eventbrite/Meetup event-listing feel
* corporate startup-board feel
* wellness clichés
* racing/checkered identity
* emoji-heavy UI

## Visual Identity

* Clean wordmark-first logo
* Simple black/white "Brielo" wordmark
* Small yellow custom dot above the "i"
* App icon uses the custom i-dot/circle symbol separately
* Yellow is the action/energy accent
* Dark mode and light mode both matter
* Use clean line icons, not native emoji icons

Avoid:

* sun icons
* spark icons
* heavy lightning/bolt branding
* checkered flags
* racing visuals
* giant standalone B icons
* overly complicated "o" customization
