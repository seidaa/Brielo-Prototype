Task:
Polish the toast/confirmation banner behavior across the Brielo prototype.

This is a small UX polish fix only.

Do not add:

* backend
* database
* auth
* WebSockets
* push notifications
* payments
* livestreaming
* AI matching
* major refactors

Do not rename folders or routes.

Problem:
The confirmation banner/toast at the top of the screen stays visible too long and blocks the app experience.

Example:
“You left the Move. Your spot is open again.”

Expected behavior:
Toasts should feel helpful but temporary.

Update toast behavior so:

* toast appears after important actions
* toast automatically disappears after about 4 seconds
* user can manually dismiss it
* user can swipe it away if the current toast system supports swipe gestures
* toast should not permanently block the top of the screen
* toast should not cover important buttons for too long

Preferred behavior:

* Auto-dismiss after 4 seconds
* Include a close/dismiss option if easy
* Support swipe-to-dismiss if supported by the existing toast component/library
* Keep styling consistent with Brielo: dark card, rounded corners, subtle border, clean text

Apply this to action confirmations like:

* “You left the Move. Your spot is open again.”
* “Move canceled. The chat is closed.”
* “You’re in. Your spot is saved.”
* “Your Move is live.”
* notification-related confirmation messages
* post-Move feedback confirmations

Do not remove the toasts entirely.
Do not make them require tapping to disappear.
Do not make them stay longer than needed.

Quality check:
After changes, test:

* join a Move
* leave a Move
* cancel a hosted Move
* create/post a Move
* submit feedback if available
* confirm each toast disappears after about 4 seconds
* confirm the user can dismiss or swipe it away if supported
* confirm Discover, Profile, Move Chat, and Show-Up Trust still work
* confirm no old Rally/Brio/Plan/Crew language is reintroduced
* confirm no emoji icons are added

Final goal:
Brielo confirmation messages should feel quick, polished, and user-controlled instead of lingering on screen too long.
