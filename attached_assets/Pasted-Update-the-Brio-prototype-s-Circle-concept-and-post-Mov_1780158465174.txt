Update the Brio prototype’s Circle concept and post-Move flow.

This is a product logic and UI copy update, not a full rebuild.

Important change:
Brio Circles should NOT be treated primarily as themed recurring group meetups.

Instead, Circles should work more like a lightweight social connection layer.

A Circle is made up of people a user has met through Moves, added after Moves, or would want to move with again.

Think of Circles as:

* people you’ve moved with
* people you’d move with again
* people you trust enough to see or join future Moves
* a simple friend/social graph layer inside Brio

Do not require every Circle to have a centralized theme like “Coffee Circle” or “Gym Circle.”
Those can exist later, but they should not be the default.

Update product language:

* Circles = your trusted Brio people / social circle
* Add to Circle = add someone after a Move
* Circle Moves = Moves visible to people in your Circle
* Public Moves = open to nearby users
* Private Moves = visible only to your Circle

Update the app so Circles feel as simple as adding friends.

POST-MOVE FLOW

After a Move ends, show a prompt:

Title:
“How was the move?”

Subtitle:
“Help Brio keep the right people moving together.”

Step 1: Reaction to the Move
Let the user choose one or more reactions:

* Good vibes
* Would do again
* Easy to show up
* Felt off
* No-show issue
* Great host
* Low pressure
* New favorite

Step 2: Attendee feedback
Show attendees from the Move with lightweight feedback options.

For each attendee, allow:

* Add to Circle
* Would move again
* Good vibes
* Didn’t show
* Felt off
* Report

Avoid harsh public star ratings.
Keep feedback private and trust/safety oriented.

Step 3: Keep it going
Ask:
“Move again?”

Options:

* Make another Move
* Invite them to a Move
* Add people to Circle
* Skip for now

Update buttons:

* “Add to Circle”
* “Would Move Again”
* “Make Another Move”
* “Invite to a Move”

PROFILE / CIRCLES SCREEN UPDATE

Change the Circles screen so it feels like a social connection page, not just recurring groups.

Sections:

1. My Circle
    People the user has added after Moves.
2. People I’d Move With Again
    Attendees the user marked positively after Moves.
3. Recent Move Connections
    People from recent Moves the user can add.

Each person card should show:

* avatar
* name/username
* level/trust badge
* last Move together
* mutual Moves if available
* button: Add to Circle / In Your Circle

Keep optional group Circles as a later feature, but make the MVP Circle concept person-based and simple.

DISCOVER UPDATE

Add filters or labels:

* Public Moves
* Circle Moves
* Moves from people in your Circle

Example copy:
“Moves from your Circle”
“People you’ve moved with before”
“Make a Move for your Circle”

SAFETY/TRUST

Post-Move feedback should support trust and safety.
If a user selects:

* Felt off
* Didn’t show
* Report

Show a simple follow-up modal:
“Tell us what happened.”

Options:

* No-show
* Creepy behavior
* Harassment
* Fake Move
* Spam/scam
* Other

After submission:
“Thanks. We’ll review this.”

Do not make negative feedback public.
Do not show public star ratings.
Use feedback internally for trust, reputation, and future safety logic.

Keep:

* React + Tailwind
* localStorage/mock data
* existing clickable prototype
* Brio branding
* Moves language
* “Make a Move”
* “Live More” tagline
* dark/light/auto theme
* no backend
* no WebSockets
* no push notifications
* no payments
* no real auth

Final goal:
Circles should feel like a natural next step after a good Move.

User flow should be:
Make or join a Move → meet people → react after the Move → add good people to your Circle → see or invite them to future Moves.
