Task:
Improve Brielo’s safety/reporting flow so pre-Move concerns and post-Move reports are logically different.

This is front-end/localStorage prototype behavior only.

Do not add:
- backend
- database
- real auth
- WebSockets
- push notifications
- payments
- livestreaming
- AI matching
- major refactors
- new major screens

Do not rename folders or routes.

Preserve:
- Brielo branding
- Moves language
- I’m In
- Request to Join
- Ask Host
- Leave Move
- Cancel Move
- Move Chat
- Show-Up Trust
- Circle
- localStorage/mock data
- current clickable flows

Problem:
The current pre-Move report flow says “Report this Move” and shows options like No-show, Harassment, Fake Move, Spam/scam, etc.

That makes more sense after a Move, but before a Move happens, the user is usually reporting that something feels suspicious, unsafe, misleading, or uncomfortable.

Goal:
Separate pre-Move safety concerns from post-Move reporting.

PART 1 — Pre-Move safety flow

On Move Detail before the Move has happened, replace or reframe the “Report this Move” action as:

“Something feels off”

Use a subtle safety link near the bottom of Move Detail.

When tapped, open a modal/sheet titled:

“Something feels off?”

Subtitle:
“Tell us what’s making you unsure. We’ll use this to review the Move.”

Options:
- Move details seem misleading
- Location feels unsafe
- Host feels suspicious
- Spam or scam
- I’m uncomfortable with this Move
- Other

After selecting an option, show a text area:

“Add a few details”

Placeholder:
“What happened or what feels off?”

Buttons:
- Cancel
- Send for Review

After submitting:
Show toast:
“Thanks. We’ll review this.”

Add a mock notification:
“Your safety note was sent for review.”

Do not call this a public report.
Do not show the report publicly.
Do not penalize anyone immediately from this pre-Move flow.

PART 2 — Post-Move reporting flow

After a Move has ended or inside the After the Move flow, keep a stronger reporting option.

Post-Move report title:
“Report this Move”

Subtitle:
“Tell us what happened. Reports are private.”

Post-Move options:
- Host didn’t show
- Attendee didn’t show
- Made me uncomfortable
- Harassment
- Fake Move
- Unsafe situation
- Spam / scam
- Other

After choosing an option, show a text area:

“Add details”

Placeholder:
“What happened?”

Buttons:
- Cancel
- Submit Report

After submitting:
Show toast:
“Thanks. We’ll review this.”

Add mock notification:
“Your report was submitted for review.”

PART 3 — Post-Move attendee-specific feedback

Inside After the Move, keep lightweight feedback separate from formal reports.

Feedback options can include:
- Good Vibes
- Would Move Again
- Great Host
- Low Pressure
- Didn’t Show
- Felt Off
- Report

Logic:
- Good Vibes affects positive mock reputation
- Would Move Again affects Circle/trust signal
- Didn’t Show affects missed Move mock data
- Felt Off should open a softer safety follow-up
- Report should open the formal post-Move report flow

Do not turn all negative feedback into public reviews.
Do not publicly shame users.
Reports and safety notes should feel private/moderation-facing.

PART 4 — Copy/tone rules

Use:
- Something feels off
- Send for Review
- Reports are private
- Thanks. We’ll review this.
- Made me uncomfortable
- Unsafe situation
- Spam / scam
- Fake Move

Avoid:
- Creepy
- Bad user
- Blacklisted
- Flake
- Public review
- Reported publicly
- Punishment language
